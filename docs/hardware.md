# Hardware

## Servos

Twelve Feetech STS3215 servos in total, all the 12 V version. The upstream bill of materials lists the 7.4 V version. We chose 12 V for both arms on purpose: with one voltage there is only one kind of power supply on the bench, so a supply can never be plugged into the wrong arm. The 7.4 V servo dies on 12 V.

The leader arm uses three gear ratios so it feels light when you move it by hand and still holds its own weight:

| Joint | Leader ratio | Follower ratio |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base pan | 1/191 | 1/345 |
| Shoulder lift | 1/345 | 1/345 |
| Elbow flex | 1/191 | 1/345 |
| Wrist flex | 1/147 | 1/345 |
| Wrist roll | 1/147 | 1/345 |
| Gripper | 1/147 | 1/345 |

## Cameras

Two 1080p USB webcams for fixed top and front views, and one wrist camera on the follower gripper. Fixed cameras must not move between recording and running the policy.

## Other

Table clamps for both bases, a small Phillips screwdriver set for M2 and M3 screws, a block and a cup for the task.
